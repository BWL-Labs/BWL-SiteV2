# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Founders and CEOs of B2B companies, mostly Series A through growth stage, deciding whether to hire a studio for brand, website, interactive assets, or sales activation. They arrive from a service page or a referral, read About to judge the people and the way of working, and either book a call or leave. Secondary: marketing and RevOps leads shortlisting vendors, who share pages internally.

## Product Purpose
Blackware Labs is a marketing studio in four parts: brand design, websites and portfolios, interactive marketing assets, and B2B sales activation (answer engine placement, Lead Detective, Mypen, Researchify, self-serve buying). It exists to turn attention into pipeline. Success for the About page is a booked call from a founder who now trusts the team and its method.

## Positioning
A studio that diagnoses before it designs: engagements open with a listening phase built on how the client's reps actually sell. The four practices are run as one system, so most clients start with one and end up using all four. Senior people do the work; there is no separate pitch team.

## Operating Context
Prospects evaluate through the marketing site (Next.js, localhost dev), service pages with live product demos, and a "Book a call" flow to /contact. Site copy uses direct, buyer-first language and a `//` monospace label idiom.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Team of six or more people. Individual names, roles, and portraits are not yet supplied; About must show clearly labelled placeholders until they are.
- Founded 2025. Based in India and Dubai, working remotely worldwide. (The current site footer/contact says "Brooklyn, NY"; the user states India and Dubai, so that older copy is stale.)
- Client names used across the site (Northbeam, Halcyon Health, Vector Freight, Kestrel Data, Orbit Payments, Fielding Labs) are sample names in demos, not confirmed public references. Do not present them as clients on About.
- No published numbers (projects shipped, clients served) are confirmed. Do not invent them.

## Brand Commitments
Name: Blackware Labs. Voice: plain, confident, buyer-first, no hype. Identity in code: ink (#080705) and paper (#FFFFFA) with gold (#E6AF2E) and rust (#C84A1F) accents, Archivo display, JetBrains Mono labels, `//` and `/ 01` label idiom, noise-textured dark panels, tilt-and-pin feature cards. Light and dark themes. Logo at public/assets/blackware-logo.svg.

## Evidence on Hand
- Service pages with working interactive demos (interactive assets, lead detective, researchify, self-serve buying, answer engine).
- Studio reel video: public/assets/studio-reel.mp4 with poster.
- Founding story: to be drafted from the positioning above and marked as a draft for the founder's review.
- Absent: team portraits, team names, client logos, testimonials, case-study numbers. Future work must label any stand-in for these as placeholder and never present it as fact.

## Product Principles
1. Prove the method, not the adjectives: show how an engagement runs before saying it is senior.
2. Every claim on the page is either true today or visibly marked as a placeholder to replace.
3. The About page ends in one action, a booked call, and never competes with the service pages for depth.
4. One system, four practices: the page should make the "start with one, use all four" path legible.
5. Two homes, remote everywhere: India and Dubai are stated plainly, and time zones are a feature for the buyer.
